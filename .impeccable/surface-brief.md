# Player surface brief

Status: implemented, code-led wrapper; no approved image comp.

Direction: game-first workbench. The live Viva La RTS iframe is the dominant surface. The owned interface comprises a compact brand/action row, remaining-height game region, status line, right-side help drawer and reload confirmation.

Visual authority: index.html and style.css; reusable design guidance in ../DESIGN.md. Third-party game visuals are outside the wrapper's design ownership.

Responsive evidence: final review images in review/desktop.png, review/desktop-help.png, review/mobile.png and review/mobile-help.png cover 1440x900 and 390x844. Final reviewer disposition supplied to documentation pass: ship. This is visual review evidence, not proof of a completed game session. Portrait gameplay remains small; the implemented copy suggests landscape and mouse/keyboard.

Composition: almost the entire viewport belongs to the game. Desktop help is at most 470px wide against the right edge; narrow-screen help fills the width. The toolbar wraps on narrow screens and keeps the action row available.

Guardrails: charcoal, panel charcoal, ivory and bronze; restrained native controls; readable Vietnamese system type; no landing grid, decorative generated art or claims of translating the game itself.
